Connect the test to the next class

A test series is most useful when each result informs a teaching decision. Give your team a repeatable workflow.

  • Plan: decide which chapters and concepts each assessment will check.
  • Prepare: create and review the questions before assigning the paper.
  • Run: set timing, access, and review rules for students.
  • Respond: review class patterns, support individual learners, and check progress on the next test.

Move from the batch to the learner

Full reporting on paid plans helps you look beyond the overall score.

  • Compare performance across the class to find chapters worth teaching again.
  • Review an individual student’s progress across tests.
  • Inspect questions and chosen options to understand repeated mistakes.
  • Use timing alongside accuracy when deciding whether the issue is understanding or pace.

Choose a plan around your actual workflow

  • Gold: unlimited students and tests, institute subdomain and logo, manual question authoring, and full reporting.
  • Platinum: adds the unlimited AI question pipeline for worked solutions and plausible distractors.
  • Diamond: adds the curated DS question bank, AITS import, study materials, and additional supervised-exam controls.
  • Compare the full features and plan details, then request a quote for your institute.

Pilot one batch before rolling out

Start with an assessment that is easy to review. Use the pilot to establish a shared routine for teachers and students.

  • Check how students sign in and find their assigned paper.
  • Confirm timing, marking, and answer-release settings.
  • Agree on which report the teaching team will review together.
  • Decide what follow-up students should receive after the test.

Questions, answered.

Can VidyaRT support JEE and NEET coaching assessments?

Yes. Teachers can build subject, chapter, and mock-test assessments for their students. Paper availability and question-bank access depend on the content and plan in use.

Can an institute use its own logo and subdomain?

Gold and higher plans include an institute subdomain and logo. See the plan comparison or contact VidyaRT to discuss setup.

How is paid pricing calculated?

Paid pricing is available on request and depends on the plan and student count. Use the contact form on the plans page to describe your institute’s requirements.
