05 / QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Short answers to what teachers ask us most.

Why don't you list prices?

Paid plans are priced on the number of students you enrol and the plan you choose, so a ten-student tutor and a five-thousand-student institute get a fair quote. Send us the form and we'll come back with one.

Is Free really free?

Yes. No card, no trial period. You get 5 students, 5 tests and 25 AI question runs for life. When they run out, everything you've made stays visible and you can upgrade whenever you're ready.

Do I lose my work when I upgrade?

No. We move your account to the new plan in place. Your tests, students, questions and results all stay where they are.

Can my students use their phones?

Students take tests in the browser at app.vidyart.online, or at your own subdomain on a paid plan. Safe Exam Browser, on Diamond, needs a supported desktop setup; we help you pilot it before a high-stakes exam.
