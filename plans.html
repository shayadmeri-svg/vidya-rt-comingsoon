05 / QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Short answers to what teachers ask us most.

Why don’t you list prices?
  • Paid pricing depends on student count and the plan you choose.
  • Send an enquiry for a quote.
Is Free really free?
  • Yes. No card and no trial period.
  • 5 students, 5 tests, and 25 AI runs are lifetime quotas.
  • Your existing work stays visible when a quota runs out.
Do I lose my work when I upgrade?
  • No. Your tests, students, questions, and results stay in place.
Can my students use their phones?
  • Students can take browser-based tests in the student app.
  • Safe Exam Browser on Diamond needs a supported setup.
  • Pilot the required exam mode on student devices first.
