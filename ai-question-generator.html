From a question to a reviewed item

Start with the concept you intend to assess. Use the AI output as a draft for teacher review.

  • Provide the question in the authoring workflow.
  • Review the proposed solution and its intermediate steps.
  • Check the wrong options and the reasoning behind them.
  • Confirm the correct answer, wording, and marking before using the item.

Make wrong options informative

A distractor is a plausible wrong answer in a multiple-choice question. Useful distractors help reveal how a learner may have approached the problem.

  • Check whether an option could come from a recognisable reasoning error.
  • Remove duplicate, ambiguous, or accidentally correct choices.
  • Avoid wording that gives away the answer without testing the concept.
  • After the test, compare the selected options with student explanations.

A quick teacher review before publishing

AI-generated content can contain errors. The final paper should reflect your subject knowledge and teaching intent.

  • Solve the question independently and verify the stated answer.
  • Check notation, units, diagrams, and any assumptions.
  • Make sure the worked solution explains the important steps.
  • Confirm that the item matches the intended chapter and difficulty.
  • Preview the question as a student will see it.
