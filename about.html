For students

For teachers and coaching institutes

The teacher experience connects paper creation, assessment controls, and performance review.

  • Build tests around the subjects and chapters you teach.
  • Use AI-assisted drafts with teacher review.
  • Choose a plan for your student count, authoring needs, and reporting.
  • Start at the teacher workspace or compare plans.

Clear expectations

VidyaRT is an independent educational platform. Practice results and reports support learning decisions.

  • VidyaRT is not an official exam authority and does not claim affiliation with NTA or the IITs.
  • A practice score or rank estimate is not an official result or an admission guarantee.
  • AI-generated content needs teacher review before use in an assessment.
  • Available features depend on the plan and assessment settings.

Questions, answered.

Are vidyart.com and vidyart.info both VidyaRT websites?

Yes. Vidyart.com explains the teacher and institution tools. Vidyart.info explains the student practice experience.

Where do I sign in?

Students sign in at app.vidyart.online. Teachers use admin.vidyart.online.

How can I contact VidyaRT about a plan?

Email info@vidyart.info or use the contact form on the plans page at vidyart.com/plans.html.
